Know Your Patterns
🌙
Late-night eating
Post-shift hunger is usually fatigue and decompression, not real hunger. Plan your post-shift option before you leave — don't decide at midnight.
🔄
Avoidance spiral
When something uncomfortable is being avoided, it grows. The smallest concrete step breaks the cycle — one call, one email, one person.
💪
This is not willpower
Your eating patterns are behavioural, not moral. Bright-line rules (trigger foods out of the house) beat in-the-moment negotiation every time.
😴
Sleep predicts overruns
Poor sleep is your strongest leading indicator of a calorie overrun. Protect sleep first, and watch the eating take care of itself.
