⚡ Highest risk window: 10pm–midnight
You've correctly identified this as your most vulnerable time. Fatigue + decompression + accessible food = compulsive eating. The strategy below removes the decision-making from that moment entirely.
The pre-shift commitment
  • 1
    Before you leave for your shift, decide: protein shake only if hungry when you get home.
    Not "something light." One specific thing. Already decided.
  • 2
    Make sure the shake powder and a glass are visible on the bench.
    Reduce friction for the right choice. Increase friction for everything else.
  • 3
    Eat your 2pm strategic snack before you leave — even if not very hungry.
    This is the upstream intervention. Less hungry at 10pm = smaller battle.
When you walk in the door
  • 1
    Large glass of water — immediately.
    Shift fatigue often feels like hunger. It's usually dehydration + cortisol.
  • 2
    Wait 20 minutes before opening the kitchen.
    Set a timer on your phone right now. Many nights the urge passes completely.
  • 3
    If still hungry after 20 minutes — protein shake only.
    Pre-decided. No negotiation. Kitchen closes after that.
  • 4
    Log the outcome in this app — resisted or acted on.
    Naming it matters. The pattern only becomes visible once it's recorded.
🚫 The trigger food conversation
Bread, crackers, cereal and ice cream are regularly coming into the house. No strategy at midnight beats having these available. This is a conversation to have with Lyalle — not about dieting, but about removing substances that trigger a compulsive cycle. Frame it that way.

The rule: active pursuit only after 10pm
Screens off or one of your three — no passive scrolling. Pre-decided before you sit down.

⚕️ Medical note — read before fasting
Your dry fast (no water) affects your medications. Speak to your GP about which to skip safely. Key concerns:

• Symbicort — skipping leaves airways unprotected. Consider taking before the fast starts.
• Tamsulosin + no fluids — can cause blood pressure drop and dizziness. Rise slowly.
• Celebrex — fine to skip one day.
• Salbutamol — keep it accessible as a reliever regardless.

Breaking the fast — recovery protocol
  • 1
    Small glass of water — sip slowly, don't gulp.
    Your stomach and kidneys need to rehydrate gradually.
  • 2
    Wait 15 minutes, then something small and easily digestible.
    Banana, small bowl of plain rice, diluted juice. Not a full meal.
  • 3
    Take your medications with food and water.
    Especially Tamsulosin and Celebrex — both need food and fluid.
  • 4
    No exercise on fast day or the following morning.
    Your body needs recovery time. Resume next day after eating normally.
  • 5
    Normal eating resumes next meal — don't "reward" with a large meal.
    Refeeding too aggressively after a dry fast stresses the system.
