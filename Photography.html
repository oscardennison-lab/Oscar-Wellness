Your Canon 6D kit — how to apply each piece to Tom Ang's tutorials.
📷
Canon 6D body
Full-frame. Excels in Tutorials 2 (exposure), 8 (available light), 9 (flash). Clean to ISO 3200 — embrace the noise for atmosphere in low-light work.
🔭
50mm f/1.8
Key lens for Tutorials 3 (tonality), 7 (timing), 14 (portrait). Natural human perspective. f/1.8 for isolation, f/8 for sharpness front-to-back.
🔍
24–105mm f/4
Tutorials 4 (zoom/prime), 13 (travel), 16 (landscape), 18 (architecture). Use 24mm for context, 105mm for compression and wildlife approach (Tut 19).
⚡
Flash
Built around Tutorial 9 (portable sunlight). Bounce for fill, off-camera for drama, drag shutter for ambient mix. Try it for Tutorial 14 portrait assignment.
📐
Two tripods
Tutorial 1 (shutter/long exposure), Tutorial 7 (timing — lock and wait). Second tripod gives you a second locked angle for architectural and landscape work.
